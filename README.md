**Data Sources**
- Exclusive Economic Zone boundaries are available to at https://marineregions.org/downloads.php#unioneezcountry. 
- Species range maps and Important Bird and Biodiversity Area (IBA) polygons can be requested at https://datazone.birdlife.org/contact-us/request-our-data. 
- Tracking data are available for request at movebank.org (dataset 3028558214) or seabirdtracking.org (datasets 783–6, 788–9, 836–40, 869, 952–4, 1424–5, 1554–6, 1585, 1617–18, 1624, 1644, 1672, 1833, 1852–53, 1855, 1935–36, 1938–39, 1941, 2087–90, 2220–21, 2223–24, 2467). 
- Protected area polygons can be requested at https://protectedplanet.net. 

Output data are available at https://birdlife-hatch.org/topics/45895/page/data-request-italy.
